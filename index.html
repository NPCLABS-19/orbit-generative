How this instrument works

Generated, never a backing track.

Drag across the pad to move between ringing tones, climbing pulses, low answers and clipped syncopation. Position latches on release. Use the arrow keys for fine adjustments, or Release pad to return to the underlying phrase. Pressure adds accent on supported devices. The tuned 808 follows the bass row with glide and a rounded pitch drop. Choose how the lead develops in Melody phrasing. Form and motif length govern how ideas repeat and change. Density sets the space between notes. Register shifts the lead by an octave. Hold repeats the current melodic bar.

The instruments are synthesized on your device. Imported audio plays as rhythmic cuts with a slowly dissolving granular tail. Captures stay in this tab. Melody sets the level of the pitched voices and chops. The audio-thread sequencer keeps timing separate from the visual display. Your effect fade controls gradual changes to stereo movement, room and echo, and the warm filter.

Tempo sets the pace. Energy shapes note density and fills. Rhythm, bass and melody balance the three musical layers. Movement opens the stereo field, space adds room and echo, and warmth rounds the filter and saturation.

These are original musical systems, influenced by warm, imperfect analog electronica, melodic progressive house and forceful techno. The Technopolis Ditty by julienp informed the study of separate synth parts and shared effects. No score or recording from that piece is included.
